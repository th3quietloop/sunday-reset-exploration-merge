Clean my room

A to-do, medium steps. 3 runs each.

How they did

  • Every model gave 7 or 8 concrete steps of 5 to 25 minutes, 85 to 120 minutes in all, and stayed consistent across three runs. Any of them would work here.
  • The best first steps came from Opus and Fable: “Grab a trash bag and a laundry basket”, 5 minutes. A start that small is what helps most with ADHD. Sonnet’s “toss all visible trash” is a quick, visible win too. Haiku starts with a 10 minute pile of clothes: fine, a bit bigger.

  • Haiku’s steps are the wordiest (“Make the bed with fresh sheets or pulled-up covers”), and one run had two overlapping trash and dish steps.

Pass the CFA Level I exam

A goal on Feb 20, 2027: its parts and hours. 3 runs each.

How they did

  • The facts to grade against: Level I has 10 topics, ethics is the biggest at 15 to 20 percent of the exam, and the usual guidance is about 300 hours. From today to Feb 20 is about 18 weeks, so roughly 17 hours a week.
  • Haiku made the mistakes that matter. Its totals were 216, 233 and 252 hours, all under the 300 it quoted itself, so the app would schedule too little. Run 1 said “plan 4 to 5 hours a week”, about a third of what’s needed. It folded practice exams into Portfolio management, gave ethics 15 hours in two runs (about 7 percent of the time), and one part name was cut short (“…and set a study”).
  • Sonnet: right total (295 to 305 hours), right weekly math (16 to 17 hours), and the most practice of any model: 55 to 65 hours of question banks and mock exams. Portfolio management is thin at 15 hours.
  • Opus: right total, right math (17 hours a week, or 2.5 a day), and it saves ethics for last, the way prep courses teach it, because ethics is memorized and weighted heavily.
  • Fable: right total and math, and the closest to the exam’s weights (ethics 30 to 35 hours, portfolio management 25), but the slowest at about 11 seconds.

Pass the bar exam

A goal on Feb 24, 2027. 1 run each.

How they did

  • The estimates are all over the place: Sonnet 312 hours, Opus 450, Fable 520, Haiku 615. Common guidance for bar prep is roughly 400 to 600 hours, so Sonnet’s plan would leave your brother short.
  • All four cover the seven multiple choice subjects. Sonnet, Opus and Fable also plan practice for the performance test, which Haiku leaves out. Fable gives the clearest weekly load, about 25 hours a week.

Fixes worth making with any model

  1. Do the math in the app. The app knows the hours and the date, so it should say “about 17 hours a week” itself instead of repeating the model’s sentence.
  2. When parts add up to less than the model’s own estimate, say so and offer to scale them up.
  3. Ask which state for a bar exam. The format now depends on the state.
  4. Give part names a little more room, or ask for shorter ones, so “set a study plan” doesn’t become “set a study”.
  5. One line in the steps instructions: include any check that could block the task, like an inspection. That closes most of Haiku’s gap on the car registration.
