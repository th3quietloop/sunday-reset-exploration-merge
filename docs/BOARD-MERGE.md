# Round two: the board, with the clean top bar

Russell's notes on round one (Oct 13): the top navigation and the icon were right. Everything under it was flipped. He wants the big board we already have (the wooden desk from 303), because those blocks make more sense and read better than the flat calendar. Focus made no sense: a six hour Work block counting down as 208:13.

Round one stays live as it was. Round two is `board/`, with its own link.

## The goal

The 303 desk, whole, under the round one top bar. The top bar is the only new chrome. Focus is rebuilt from what it's for.

## The long list

### The top bar
1. The top bar from round one sits over the desk: the three brick icon, Sunday Reset, Sample, then Today, Calendar, To-dos, Focus, then the Sunday Reset button and the initial for Settings.
2. Today is the desk's Today: the band, the day board alone, Add.
3. Calendar is the desk's Planner: the date card, Day, Week, Month, Year, the tray, kinds of day. Calendar is the plain word for it.
4. To-dos is the Planner with To do open and what's due laid out on the bench beside the day, the same thing the notepad does.
5. Focus is its own room (below).
6. The Today and Planner switch goes away. The top bar is the one way between them, so there aren't two.
7. T, C, and F keys follow the top bar. P still opens the Calendar, for the hands that know it.
8. One Sunday Reset button, in the top bar. It's black on the days it's due (Sunday, and Monday or Tuesday until the week is set), says Catch up this week on Wednesday and Thursday when that's what it does, and is quiet the rest of the week. The tray's copy and Today's stacked copy go.
9. The initial at the right opens Settings. The date card's kicker stays as the app's name and the clock, not a hidden button.
10. The top bar is white and the desk is wood, so the line between chrome and board is obvious.
11. The desk frames itself under the bar: nothing on the board, and no prop, hides behind it.
12. Every panel that used to start at the top of the window (the date card, the band, the side panel, Settings, the list, quick add, the preview bar) starts under the bar.
13. A new visitor lands on the sample board, not the welcome. Set up my week is still in Settings.
14. On a phone the bar keeps the four words and the round reset button; the phone's own list and day sit under it.

### Focus, rebuilt
What was wrong: it counted seconds down from the whole length of whatever you picked, so Work read 208:13. Minutes over 60 in a clock face, seconds nobody needs, and no idea what "focus on Work" means for six hours.

15. Focus is a session, not a block. You pick what, then how long.
16. What: the block you're in, or a to-do (what's due first, then the rest), or a few words of your own.
17. When the block you're in is long (Work, a creative afternoon), it asks what part of it: a to-do, or the block itself.
18. How long is three answers with their end times written out: 25 min (until 2:15 pm), 50 min (until 2:40 pm), and Until 4:45 pm, the end of the block you're in. A to-do also offers its own length.
19. Time reads the way people say it: 18 min left, until 2:15 pm. Never seconds, never 208 minutes, never a clock face over an hour.
20. The last minute says Less than a minute.
21. The session is a brick in the thing's own color, worn down to now, the same way the band wears the block you're in. One visual language for time everywhere.
22. Three buttons while it runs: Pause (Resume), 5 more min, Done. Leaving is the x, Back to the board.
23. When the time's up, a soft chime and one question: Done, or 10 more minutes.
24. Done says what happened in words: 25 min on Renew passport. A to-do that was the focus is checked off, through the board, so the badge, the notepad, and To-dos all agree. Undo is in the toast.
25. After Done, three ways on: Take a 5 min break, the next thing (the next due to-do, named), Back to the board.
26. A break is a session too, in Breathing room's color, and it ends with Back to it.
27. The session survives a reload and a trip to the Calendar. The Focus tab shows a small dot while one is running, and the tab's title says what's left.
28. Nothing else is on screen in Focus: the board dims and blurs behind it, so it's still yours but it's quiet.
29. No streaks, no scores, no totals for the day. Calm, plain, specific.

### Menus that get to the point on the board
30. One strong button per screen. In Calendar that's the Sunday Reset button when it's due.
31. Hover hints stay hints: the keys only show on hover, as they do now.
32. The list of kinds of day stays one dropdown in the date card.
33. Labels stay on the nose: Calendar, To-dos, Focus, Block library.
34. No new color in the chrome. Color stays the blocks'.
35. Round one is still there to flip to, at its own link.
