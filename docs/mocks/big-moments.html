V11-2 mock

Big moments: see the distance, do the next step

Six screens, each about 390 wide, so they work on a phone and in the desk's side panel. Everything here comes from what V11 already knows; what's new is how it shows.

  • Steps are blocks you can drag, with one circle that means Done.
  • Next moves down the list as you finish things.
  • The countdown comes in four shapes: a trip, an exam, a fuzzy date, and no date at all.
  • Only the next step is loud. The rest stays folded.
1. Steps are blocksEach step looks like the block it becomes, with the grip V11's bricks use. The circle beside it only means Done. Next moves to the first step that's left. The two corner buttons sit in one row and never touch.
