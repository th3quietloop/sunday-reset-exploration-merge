Corners (V1) stays as it is, at its own link. V2 is `v/corners2`.

## The list

### The corners
1. Top left: hours yours this week (or today, in the day view).
2. Top middle: back, This week (Oct 11 to 17), forward. In the day view the kind of day sits beside it.
3. Top right: Today, Day, Week, Month, Year, and beside it your account: a circle with your initial and your name, the way most apps tuck settings in.
4. Bottom left: Sunday Reset, only that. Gold when it's due, quiet the rest of the week.
5. Bottom right: Add, To-dos (with what's due as a count), Block library.
6. In Today the band takes the top, and the account stays where it always is.

### Block library, on the desk
7. Block library opens the 3D rack beside the board: in the day view it's the rack beside the day, in the week it's the bench beside the week. Never a flat tray.
8. Pressing it again puts the rack away. The button looks pressed while it's out.
9. From the month or the year, Block library steps in to the week first, since the rack lives beside a week or a day.
10. The flat tray bar never shows in V2, except as the Sunday Reset's steps and Today's dock.

### To-dos, as stacks that hold still
11. To-dos opens the card sheet from Library: Due soon, each list, New to-do, and below them Dinners and time together.
12. The sheet is one fixed size. Filters change what's in it, never its width or height.
13. The cards sit in a fixed grid, five across, all the same size, so a filter with two cards doesn't stretch them.
14. Only the grid scrolls; the title, filters, and Find stay put.
15. Picking a card lays its pieces out on the bench beside the board, in 3D, like Block library. Everything you can place comes from the right side of the desk.
16. The sheet blurs the board behind it, as every pop-up does.

### Settings you can find
17. The account chip shows your initial and your first name, with a chevron.
18. It opens a small menu: you at the top with Settings as the first thing, then Go to (Today, Calendar, To-dos, Block library, Dinners, time together, Focus, the week as a list), then How it works and Sound.
19. Esc or a tap outside closes it, and focus goes back to the chip.

### Color
20. A mark on gold is drawn in espresso, never white.
21. Pressed things are cream with espresso type; everything else is cream on espresso.

### The clock: a Pomodoro Easter egg
22. Point at the clock and it says "Tap for a Pomodoro timer".
23. Tap it: the camera leans in toward the clock, and a small timer card opens beside it.
24. Presets: 25 and 5, 50 and 10, or your own focus and break lengths, in 5 minute steps.
25. It counts down in minutes and seconds (it's a timer you watch, so seconds belong here), with four dots for the rounds and a long break after the fourth.
26. Start, Pause, Skip, Reset. A soft chime at each switch between focus and break.
27. While it runs, the hours chip on the top left becomes the timer, with a red dot. Tap it to bring the card back. The tab's title shows the time.
28. It survives a reload.
29. Closing it lets the camera settle back. Esc closes it too.
30. Focus (the session for one thing) stays where it was: F, and the account menu.
